          Small things that can help

          • Have a glass of water, or something warm to hold.
          • Sit somewhere familiar — a favourite chair, a window, a doorstep.
          • Reach out to someone. A single word is enough.
          • Eat something small, if you can.
          • Step outside for a minute of air.

          Support

          Griefcase is a personal keepsake, not therapy or medical care. Grief doesn’t follow a schedule, and you don’t have to be in crisis to deserve support.

          If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

          • NHS: grief after bereavement or loss (opens in a new tab) — general information (UK).
          • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (opens in a new tab) — call or text 988 in the US and its territories only. Elsewhere, look for a local service.
